About

Clear news. Calm delivery.

GoPriLad is a small, independent project built around a simple idea: staying informed should not feel like drinking from a firehose.

What GoPriLad is

GoPriLad collects public world-news headlines from established publishers and presents them in one readable place. We link to the original reporting so you can read the full story at the source.

We do not pretend to replace those publishers, and we do not republish their articles as our own.

What we send

If you create an account and explicitly opt in, we may send occasional GoPriLad news emails. Marketing consent is optional and unchecked by default. You can use the site without receiving a single email.

Every account must confirm its email address before sign-in. Every marketing email includes a working unsubscribe path. Suppressed and unsubscribed addresses stay suppressed.

How we handle your details

We store your email address, a securely hashed password, and the consent records needed to prove what you agreed to. We do not sell your email address. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

Read the privacy policy for the full detail, including your rights and how to contact us.

The short version

  • Public news feeds, clearly attributed
  • A free account, verified by email
  • News email is opt-in only
  • Unsubscribe and suppression are honoured
  • No ad tracking on this site
